Building an eSIM and virtual number business from scratch means years of work: wallets, providers, failover, refunds, an admin panel. **Naara White Label** gives you the platform to run under your own brand.

## What you get

A complete, installable version of the platform with your name, colours and domain. It installs on a VPS or on shared cPanel hosting, like a normal software product, and ships with safe defaults so a fresh install is usable the same day.

## How buying works

You choose a licence plan, check the price and pay by wallet, card or an approved manual method. You receive a licence key, install the package, enter the key to activate, and your install starts. A resume widget remembers where you stopped if you leave halfway.

## Custom orders

If a plan is not quite right, you can send a custom request. The team replies with a quote you can accept and pay inside the same flow.

## What stays with Naara

Your install runs your business. Things that are about overseeing other installs, for example the licence issuer, stay with the main platform. We keep that line sharp so a customer of yours never meets anything that is not theirs.

## What you bring

Your own provider accounts and payment gateways. The platform connects to them, but the relationships and the balances are yours.

## Before you buy

Read the plans, ask for the installation guide, and decide where you will host. The documentation explains how updates arrive and how to roll back safely.

## Questions people ask

**Can I change the logo and colours?**
Yes. Your install carries your name, colours and domain, and the setup guides you through them.

**How do updates work?**
Updates are delivered as signed packages you apply safely, with a rollback path if something goes wrong.

**What does it cost to run?**
Hosting, your provider balances and payment gateway fees. The licence plan is listed with its price before you buy.
