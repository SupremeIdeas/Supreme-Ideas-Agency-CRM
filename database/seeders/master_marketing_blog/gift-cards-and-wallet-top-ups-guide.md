Your Naara wallet is one balance you use for eSIM data, numbers, gift cards and more. A few simple habits make it painless.

## Topping up

Open the wallet and choose **Top Up**. Depending on what is switched on for your country, you can pay with a card, a bank transfer or crypto. The credit lands as soon as the payment is confirmed by the provider, not before.

## Two currencies

Your balance is in US dollars with a live local equivalent underneath. If you top up in naira, you see exactly what it converts to, and the rate is the one in force at that moment.

## Spending

Every purchase shows the final price before you confirm. You pay the retail price shown; nothing is added at the end. Each purchase writes a line in your history with your balance before and after.

## Gift cards

**Naara Gift** lets you buy gift cards with your wallet balance, from the same account. Brand logos and details are fetched and checked before they appear.

## Withdrawing earnings

If you earn from referrals, rewards or your brand, you can send earnings to a bank account you have saved and verified. New bank details take a verification step first, to protect you.

## Sharing

You can let a family member or teammate spend from your balance with a limit you set, and remove them whenever you like.

## If a payment is stuck

Check the transaction history first. If a payment shows pending for long, message Support with the reference. A charge that does not result in delivery is refunded automatically, not by chasing.

## Questions people ask

**Why is a top-up pending?**
The provider has not confirmed it yet. Bank transfers and crypto take longer than cards. It credits automatically on confirmation.

**Can I get a refund to my card?**
Refunds go back to the wallet by default. Ask Support if you need a different route and have the reference ready.

**Are gift cards refundable?**
Digital gift cards are usually final once delivered. A card that fails to deliver is refunded automatically.
