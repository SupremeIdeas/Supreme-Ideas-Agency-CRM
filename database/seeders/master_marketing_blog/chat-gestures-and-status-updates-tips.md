Good chat apps are full of small shortcuts. Here are the ones in Naara Chat that people often discover late.

## Swipe, tap and hold

Swipe a message to reply to it. Double-tap to react. Press and hold for more options. Press and hold the microphone to record a voice note, slide to lock it so your thumb is free, or slide away to cancel.

## Make them yours

**Chat gestures** lets you choose what each gesture does, how long a press takes, or turn gestures off. A sample message on that page lets you try changes right away.

## Updates

The **Updates** tab shows status updates from the people you are connected with. You can post a photo or a short video from the in-app camera, and mention people so they get a nudge. Brands on Naara Pro can post updates too.

## The send menu

Press and hold send for options: send silently so nobody's phone buzzes, send so the message disappears, or schedule it for later. It is handy for early-morning messages across time zones.

## Find things again

Star the messages you may need later, search inside a chat, and pull down on the list to search all chats. Select several chats at once to pin, mute or archive them together.

## Slash and emoji menus

Type a slash for quick actions. Type a colon and a word for an emoji. On a computer, there are keyboard shortcuts for the common moves.

## Photos and videos

Open an image to view it full screen and swipe between pictures from the same chat. Share contacts and locations from the attach menu.

## Take the tour

The guided tours for Naara Chat point at the real buttons, one by one, and you can replay them from the help hub.

## Questions people ask

**Can I turn off swipe actions?**
Yes, in Chat gestures. You can also change what each gesture does.

**Who sees my status update?**
People you are connected with, according to your privacy settings.

**How do I recall a scheduled message?**
Open the scheduled item and cancel it before it sends.
