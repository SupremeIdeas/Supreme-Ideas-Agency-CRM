Everyone has met a chatbot that invents a refund policy. The damage is not the wrong answer. It is the confident tone. Respond was built around a different rule: **if your own knowledge does not say it, the assistant does not say it.**

## Grounded, not guessing

You teach the assistant with FAQs, notes and pages from your website. When a customer asks something, it first searches that material. If nothing relevant is found, the model is not even asked. The conversation moves to your team with a private note explaining why, and, in Auto mode, the customer gets one short message saying a person will reply.

## It knows when to step back

The assistant hands over, without trying to answer, when a customer asks for a person, complains, or raises something sensitive such as fraud or legal threats. You can edit that list of words. It also hands over when someone tries to change its instructions, for example by writing "ignore your rules".

## Limits you control

- **Mode.** Off, Suggest (it drafts, you approve) or Auto.
- **Daily limit.** A hard stop on how many AI replies run each day.
- **Monthly allowance.** Part of your Naara Pro plan, shared with the inbox assistant.
- **One decision per message.** A repeated delivery never produces two replies.

## Honest by design

The assistant is told to say it is an AI if asked, to reply in the customer's language, never to invent prices or links, never to promise refunds or discounts, and never to ask for passwords, card numbers or one-time codes. It treats the customer's text as a question, never as orders.

## What you can see

The AI assistant tab lists every recent decision: answered, suggested, handed over, refused, or stopped by a limit, with the reason. The cost of each reply is measured in the platform's AI cost log, so a plan's margin is never a guess.

## How to roll it out

Start with Suggest for a week. Read what it drafts. Fix the FAQ wherever a draft is wrong. When most drafts are ones you would have sent, try Auto for a single channel.

## Questions people ask

**How many FAQs do I need before turning it on?**
Three good ones is enough to start in Suggest mode. Add more wherever a draft is wrong or the assistant hands over.

**Can it speak other languages?**
It replies in the language the customer wrote in, but it can only use facts from your knowledge, so add key policies in the languages your customers use.

**Does it see my customers' private data?**
It reads the conversation and your knowledge. It cannot look up accounts, send money or change anything.
