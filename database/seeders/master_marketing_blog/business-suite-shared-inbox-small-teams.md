Small teams do not usually fail at support because they do not care. They fail because the conversation lives on one person's phone.

## What the Business Suite gives a team

- A **workspace** with one shared wallet and seats for your people.
- **Roles**: owner, admin, agent and viewer, so everyone sees only what they need.
- A **shared inbox** where messages from your channels land in one list.
- **Call flows** for menus, ring groups, business hours and voicemail.
- **Recording** with a clear notice for every caller, plus review and deletion under your own retention rules.
- An **AI voice assistant** that can answer a number, take a callback request and hand over to a person.
- **Integrations**: a workspace API key and signed webhooks.

## Why roles matter

Agents answer customers. Admins connect channels and change settings. Viewers watch without changing anything. The owner holds the plan. Only owners and admins can connect channels or open the automation settings, so a new agent cannot break your setup by accident.

## A conversation always has an owner

Open conversations, waiting ones and finished ones are separate lists. You can assign a conversation to yourself, filter to **Mine** or **Unassigned**, and tick several to finish or take them together. A yellow **Overdue** tag appears if a customer has waited more than fifteen minutes.

## Notes and saved replies

Leave a private note on a conversation for your team. Save the replies you send every day, and insert them with one tap.

## Start small

Create the workspace, connect one channel, invite one teammate. The **Setup guide** on the overview page remembers where you stopped and tells you what each step enables.

## Questions people ask

**Can two people answer the same customer by accident?**
Assigning a conversation to yourself marks it as yours. Filter to Unassigned to pick up what nobody owns yet, so work is not duplicated.

**Do viewers see everything?**
Viewers can read what the role allows but cannot reply or change settings. Roles are checked on the server, not just hidden in the screen.

**What if a teammate leaves?**
Remove them from Team. Their seat is freed at once, and their conversations can be reassigned to someone else.
