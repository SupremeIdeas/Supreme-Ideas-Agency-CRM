Following a brand usually means leaving your chat app, finding their page, tapping follow and hoping you remember to come back. In Naara Chat the brands are **inside People**, one tab away from your friends.

## Where to find it

Open **People** and choose **Brands**. You can also come straight from the chat screen: the **Invite** button has a shortcut called *Find and connect with brands*.

## Three ways to look

- **Discover** shows brands you have not connected with yet. Search by name or topic, or pick a category.
- **Connected** lists the brands you already follow.
- **Earn credits** shows brands where you still have pages left to follow. Each real follow on a brand's social page earns NaaraCredits.

## Quick connect

At the top of Discover there is a strip of popular brands. Each card has one **Connect** button. That is the whole step.

## Connect and message

If a brand accepts messages from you, its card shows **Connect and message**. One tap connects you and opens the chat, so you can say hello straight away. Some brands keep chat for connected people only. In that case you still connect, and the chat opens once you are connected with each other.

## Why brands like it

A brand that joins Naara Pro gets a listing with its real social handles, a verified seal, a business card with a QR code and short link, and a chat inbox. Followers are real people who chose to connect, and every follow counts toward the monthly promise the plan makes.

## Safety, briefly

Brand website links are checked so visitors only meet safe pages. You can leave a brand any time by tapping **Connected**, and you can block or report a chat like any other.

## Try it

Open the Brands tab, connect with one brand you genuinely like, and follow its pages. Then check **Earn credits** to see what you collected.

## Questions people ask

**Will connecting spam me?**
No. Connecting means you follow the brand inside Naara. A brand can reply to you, and you can disconnect with one tap.

**Why does a brand show 'chat opens once you are connected with each other'?**
That brand keeps chat for connections only. Connect with it, and once it connects back (or the brand accepts), the chat opens.

**How are the follows checked?**
Follows on social pages are verified before credits are granted, so repeating follow and unfollow does not earn twice.
