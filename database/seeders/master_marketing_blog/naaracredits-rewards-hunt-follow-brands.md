NaaraCredits are the small currency that makes Naara feel generous. One of the easiest ways to earn them is also one of the most useful: following brands you actually like.

## How the Hunt works

A brand lists its social handles on its profile. Each handle you follow earns a reward, set by the brand's plan. Connect with the brand in Naara Chat first, then follow its pages. The **Earn credits** view shows what is left to follow and what you can still collect.

## It has to be real

Follows are verified, not trusted blindly. Following and unfollowing repeatedly does not earn twice. Brand links are checked so the pages you land on are safe.

## What credits are for

Credits pay for Naara Chat features such as calls and some media. Naara Pro plans can include a monthly refill. You keep control: you choose whether chat is funded by credits or by your wallet.

## Other ways to earn

The Rewards screen lists daily check-ins, referrals and goals on your journey. Goals are switched on by the platform, so the list you see is the list that is live.

## Tips

- Connect, then follow all the pages, because the reward is per handle.
- Do the check-in daily. Small amounts add up.
- Use credits for calls so your wallet stays for plans and numbers.

## For brands

Every real follow counts toward your monthly promise. Share your business card or QR code, and your short link brings people straight to your profile.

## Questions people ask

**Do credits expire?**
Check the Rewards screen for the current rules. Plan refills and rewards are shown with their terms.

**Can I withdraw credits as cash?**
Credits are for spending on Naara features. Cash earnings from referrals or your brand are a separate balance you can withdraw to a verified bank account.

**Why did a follow not count?**
Follows are verified. If a page was already followed, was private, or was unfollowed again, it will not earn twice.
