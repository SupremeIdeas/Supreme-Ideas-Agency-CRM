Some questions deserve more than a blog post. How do you study in the UK as someone from Africa? What does renting there really cost, and what are the traps? The **Handbook Store** exists for those questions.

## What a handbook is

A handbook is a book-length guide you can read in the app or download as an **EPUB or PDF**. Each one is written for readers whose journey starts in Africa, with checklists, worked examples (clearly marked as examples), and a note of what changed recently.

## Our standard

We correct, then expand. Every fee, date and rule is checked against official sources and marked with the date of the book. Anything we cannot confirm is flagged for verification rather than guessed. Photos are real, from open sources, kept light so pages load quickly, and credited on a photo credits page.

## Reading, your way

The reader has chapters you can jump between, adjustable text, a table of contents and a **read-aloud** mode. Your library keeps what you own and where you stopped.

## Included with Naara Pro

When your plan includes handbooks, you unlock a set number of titles while the plan is active. You can also buy titles on their own. If something is wrong with a purchase, there is a refund request you can submit from your library.

## Titles you will find

The first books cover **studying in the UK** and **renting in the UK**, with more being prepared. A title is only published when its checklist passes: the EPUB, the cover, the back cover and the price.

## A tip

Start with the preview chapter, then read the checklist at the end of the part you need. The checklists are designed to be printed.

## Questions people ask

**Can I read on my phone and my laptop?**
Yes. Your library follows your account and remembers where you stopped. You can also download the EPUB or PDF.

**Why do some books say 'verify'?**
Because rules change. We flag anything that must be rechecked before you rely on it, with the date the book was written.

**Do I lose a book if my plan ends?**
Books unlocked through a plan stay tied to the plan. Books you bought separately stay yours.
