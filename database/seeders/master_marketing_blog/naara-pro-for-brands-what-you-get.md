Plans are easiest to judge when you can see everything on one page. Naara Pro is built that way: one package, one comparison, and the same list everywhere you look.

## The package in five parts

1. **Brand listing.** Your social handles, a verified Naara Pro seal, a business card with a QR code and a short link, and a safe link to your website.
2. **Naara Chat.** Photos, videos, voice notes and files in chat, status updates, and being reachable by anyone on Naara.
3. **Handbooks.** Travel handbooks included while your plan is active.
4. **Business Suite.** A team workspace, shared inbox, call flows, recording with caller notices, an AI voice assistant, an inbox assistant and an API with webhooks.
5. **Perks.** A monthly NaaraCredits refill and premium dashboard skins.

## What changes from plan to plan

Some things are simply on for every plan. Others scale: how many social handles you list, the follower promise per handle each month, how many video previews you can add, and how many NaaraCredits arrive each month. A few Business Suite modules are switched on per plan by the platform, so a plan only shows what it truly includes.

## The follower promise

Each handle carries a monthly follower guarantee. If a brand falls short, its listing is boosted until it catches up. The numbers are on the plan card, not buried in terms.

## What we do not do

We do not list features that are switched off today, and we do not promise results we cannot control. If a module is not live on this install, it is not on the page.

## If your plan ends

Nothing is deleted. A workspace whose plan lapses is paused, and everything returns when the plan does.

## Getting started

Open **Get Listed**, follow the brand wizard (links, connections, chat profile, plan, share), and start with the plan that matches your handles. You can move up later and the page shows exactly what the move adds.

## Questions people ask

**Which plan should a small brand start with?**
Start with the plan that covers the handles you actually post on. You can move up later, and the plan page shows exactly what the next step adds.

**Can I try before I pay?**
Where a trial is offered it is shown in the brand wizard, with the end date and what happens after it.

**Do followers have to pay anything?**
No. Following a brand is free, and followers earn NaaraCredits for verified follows.
