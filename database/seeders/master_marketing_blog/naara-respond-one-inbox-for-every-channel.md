Customers do not pick the channel that is convenient for you. They write where they already are. Naara Respond puts those messages in one inbox so your team does not have to chase them.

## Channels that work today

- **Telegram.** Create a bot with BotFather, paste its token in Channels, and messages arrive in Respond.
- **Website chat.** Add the website address you want the chat bubble on, choose a greeting and colour, then paste one line of code into your site.
- **Email.** Forward your support email to the address shown on the card. Replies leave from your own support address.

WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger follow once Meta's approvals are in place. We would rather say that plainly than promise a date we cannot control.

## The inbox

Search, filter by status, and see who is handling what. The thread shows each message with its delivery status. On channels with a 24-hour rule, the composer tells you when the window has closed instead of letting a message fail silently.

## Teach it once

In **Automation > Knowledge** you add FAQs, policies and pages from your website. The assistant answers only from that material.

## Let it help, safely

The AI assistant has three modes. **Off** leaves everything to your team. **Suggest** drafts a reply and you press *Use this reply*. **Auto** answers by itself. It hands over to a person if it does not know, if a customer asks for a human or raises a sensitive topic, and if someone tries to change its instructions. It stops at a daily limit and at your plan's monthly allowance.

## Flows and broadcasts

Flows react to a first message, a keyword or the time of day. Broadcasts go only to people who agreed to hear from you, and anyone who writes STOP is removed immediately.

The **Insights** tab shows how fast you reply, what the assistant handled and whether each channel is healthy, with a plain green, amber or red.

## Questions people ask

**Do I need a developer to add website chat?**
No. You paste one line of code before the closing body tag. If you use WordPress, add it with any plugin that inserts a header or footer script.

**Can customers see who answered?**
They see your business name and greeting. Whether a person or the assistant answered is your choice to show; the assistant says it is an AI if a customer asks.

**What if the queue worker stops?**
Messages are stored first and processed after, so nothing is lost. The Insights page turns red and says incoming messages are waiting, and they flow again when the worker returns.
