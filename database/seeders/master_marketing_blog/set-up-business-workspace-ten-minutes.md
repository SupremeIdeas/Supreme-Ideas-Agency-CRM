Nobody wants a settings maze on a Monday morning. The Business Suite **Setup Guide** is a short, resumable pop-up that takes you from an empty workspace to one that answers customers.

## Start it

Open the Business overview and press **Start setup** (it says **Continue setup** once you have begun). A progress bar shows how far you are, and each step shows what it enables before it asks for anything.

## The steps, in order

1. **Business profile.** A name customers will recognise, and a business email. We ask you to confirm the email so notices reach a real inbox.
2. **Connect a channel.** Telegram, WhatsApp or Instagram, with plain instructions and a *Test and connect* button that checks your details with the channel before saving them.
3. **Teach and automate (Respond).** Add a FAQ, then choose **Suggest** so the assistant drafts and you approve. Turn on a starter flow if you like.
4. **Team.** Invite colleagues as admin, agent or viewer. You can skip this and come back.
5. **Call flows, recording, AI voice, integrations.** Each appears only if your plan includes it. A locked step tells you what unlocks it.

## What the guide never does

It never shows a step as done because a box was ticked. Each status is read from what is really saved, so the overview and the wizard cannot disagree. Secrets you paste (like a bot token) are encrypted, wiped from the page after connecting and never shown again.

## If something goes wrong

Each step explains the likely cause in words and offers a one-click fix where there is one. A channel that needs attention says so on the overview.

## Ten-minute plan

- Minutes 0 to 2: name and email.
- Minutes 2 to 5: connect one channel and send a test message.
- Minutes 5 to 8: add three FAQs.
- Minutes 8 to 10: set the assistant to Suggest and answer one real question with it.

## Questions people ask

**Can I skip steps?**
Yes. Optional steps say so, and you can skip any step and return later. The overview shows what is left.

**Why is a step locked?**
Your plan does not include that feature yet. The step says which feature unlocks it.

**Is my business email shown to customers?**
It receives workspace notices and builds trust. The name is what customers see.
